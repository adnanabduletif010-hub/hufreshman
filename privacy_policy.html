Privacy Policy

Application ID: com.curiovana.hufreshman  •  Haramaya University Students Only  •  Last Updated: October 2026
Academic Application Notice: HU Freshman is an educational mobile application designed for Haramaya University freshman and continuing students.

HU Freshman the app is an educational platform built for Haramaya University (HU) Students in Ethiopia. It provides past university exam preparation materials, academic questions, solutions, study plans, and campus directories tailored to the Haramaya University curriculum. We are committed to protecting user privacy and complying with Google Play Developer Policies and Data Safety requirements.

1. Intended Audience & Academic Services

HU Freshman is designed exclusively for Haramaya University (HU) students. Only current or incoming Haramaya University students are permitted to create an account and access the full features of this app. It provides educational resources, past exam materials, and study tools specifically aligned with the Haramaya University academic program. Registration features are for Haramaya University Student Community Membership and directory verification to confirm active HU affiliation and facilitate peer academic discussions.

2. Information We Collect

When you register for an account or use HU Freshman, we collect the following limited information:

  • Personal Identification: Full Name, University Name, and Academic Year to personalize your student profile.
  • Contact & Authentication: Phone number and an account password used to authenticate your student membership session.
  • Community Content: Text questions, posts, and comments you submit within the student community discussion board.
  • Local Learning Data: Bookmarked questions and test practice scores stored locally on your device for revision.

3. Data Sharing & Service Providers

We do NOT sell, rent, license, or monetize student personal data with third-party advertisers, data brokers, or marketing networks. We only process and transfer data to trusted cloud infrastructure providers (specifically Google Firebase for authentication, database hosting, and crash diagnostics) strictly to deliver and secure our app services.

4. Data Security & Storage

We implement standard technical and organizational security measures to protect user data against unauthorized access, loss, or disclosure. All network communications use standard HTTPS / TLS encryption in transit.

5. User Rights & Account Deletion

In accordance with Google Play User Data policies, users have full rights to delete their account and associated personal data at any time:

  • In-App Deletion: You can permanently erase your account, registration records, and local data instantly by navigating to the Profile tab and selecting "Delete Account & Data".
  • Web / Remote Deletion Request: If you cannot access the app, you may request full deletion of your account by sending an email with your registered phone number to cruad67@gmail.com. Upon receipt, your account, personal identification, and associated data will be permanently wiped from our database within 30 days.

6. Children's Privacy

Our application is intended for college and university students (aged 18 and older). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13.
