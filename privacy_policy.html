Privacy Policy

Application ID: com.curiovana.hufreshman  •  Last Updated: September 2026
Free Academic Application Notice: HU Freshman is a completely free-to-access educational mobile application designed for Ethiopian university freshman students. The application does not sell digital in-app content, premium files, or digital subscriptions.

HU Freshman ("we", "our", or "the app") provides past university exam preparation materials, academic questions, solutions, and campus directories. We are committed to protecting user privacy and complying with Google Play Developer Policies and Data Safety requirements.

1. Free Service & Student Membership Registration

HU Freshman is distributed as a free educational app. Any registration features in the app are strictly for Student Campus Organization Membership and directory verification to confirm active university affiliation and facilitate student community peer discussions. Membership registration is NOT an in-app purchase of digital content, locked files, or paid features.

2. Information We Collect

When you register for student membership or use HU Freshman, we collect the following limited information:

  • Personal Identification: Full Name, University Name, and Academic Year to personalize your student profile.
  • Contact & Authentication: Phone number and an account password used solely to authenticate your student membership session.
  • Membership Verification Reference: Optional membership transaction reference numbers provided solely for manual record-keeping and verifying physical campus club membership enrollment. This reference is NOT used to grant access to in-app content, academic materials, or any digital features. The app and all its content are free.
  • Local Learning Data: Bookmarked questions and test practice scores stored locally on your device for revision.

3. Zero Third-Party Sharing

We do NOT sell, rent, license, or monetize any student personal data with third-party advertisers, data brokers, or commercial marketing networks. All collected information is strictly utilized to authenticate campus membership and maintain academic platform integrity.

4. Data Security & Storage

We implement standard technical and organizational security measures to protect user data against unauthorized access, loss, or disclosure. All network communications use standard HTTPS / TLS encryption in transit.

5. User Rights & Account Deletion

In accordance with Google Play User Data policies, users have the right to request deletion of their account and data at any time:

  • In-App Deletion: You can permanently erase your account, registration records, and local data at any time directly via the "Delete Account & Data" button in the Profile tab.
  • External Request: You can request full deletion of your records by emailing cruad67@gmail.com.

6. Children's Privacy

Our application is intended for college and university students (aged 18 and older). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13.
